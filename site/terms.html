Purpose and limitations

GlideHorizon is not a certified or flight-qualified instrument. Do not use it as a primary or emergency attitude reference, or as a means of flying without external visual reference. A plausible-looking horizon can be wrong.

This website describes an experimental Android application and its documented features. It does not certify a device, installation or build for flight use. Review the full operational limitations and perform configuration and checks on the ground.
