Understand the limits

Operational limitations

A plausible-looking horizon can be wrong. A healthy sensor status is not proof of accurate attitude.

Not a flight-qualified instrument

GlideHorizon is experimental software, not a certified or flight-qualified instrument. Do not use it as a primary or emergency attitude reference, or as a means of flying without external visual reference.

Perform installation, mounting, calibration and configuration on the ground. Do not let operating or adjusting the app distract from operating an aircraft.

Acceleration, drift and mounting

The app estimates attitude from a phone's gyroscope and accelerometer. Accelerometers cannot distinguish gravity from sustained vehicle acceleration. Forward acceleration may appear as pitch; turning can distort roll. Coordinated aircraft turns do not eliminate this limitation.

Reducing accelerometer correction trades acceleration-induced error against gyro drift. No correction-gain setting guarantees accurate aircraft attitude. Calibration establishes a mounting reference; it does not remove these limitations or qualify the app for flight use.

Check mounting, orientation, the known stationary reference and displayed response during controlled ground tests. A changed mounting may require new calibration. See mounting and calibration.

Know what each display means

  • Speed is GPS ground speed, not airspeed. Coloured speed references must not be used to decide stall, manoeuvring or overspeed safety.
  • The grid is a visual reference plane. It is not terrain, synthetic vision or ground-clearance information.
  • Altitude and flight level are experimental. Phone pressure/GPS indications do not replace approved aircraft altimetry. QNH does not adjust GPS altitude.
  • Magnetic heading is not true heading or GPS track. Metal, magnets and electrical equipment can affect it. The dedicated compass is not a separate heading sensor or independent backup. It shows MAG UNAVAILABLE and dashes without a magnetic reference, never substituting relative turn. MAG / GYRO HOLD means propagation from an established magnetic reference, not an assurance of accuracy. On the main horizon's heading bar, relative turn may drift; relative zero is not north.
  • The turn instrument is not independent. Turn-rate calculation shares the attitude estimate and its limitations. The slip ball indicates filtered specific force, not aerodynamic sideslip angle.

Stale values and connected displays

When attitude becomes unusable, the display can retain a previously usable sensor frame for up to five seconds. The dedicated turn-and-slip page also holds briefly when turn-rate calculation becomes unavailable. ATTITUDE HELD or DISPLAY HELD shows the original measurement's increasing age. Retained attitude, slip, compass and other indications are old data, not current measurements.

Continuous faults do not restart the hold timer. Usable data resumes when it returns; otherwise unavailable readings are cleared after the hold limit. No previous usable frame means no invented display. Independent pressure/GPS failures retain their own freshness checks. Stale or unavailable indications must not be interpreted as live measurements.

The DEGRADED label is omitted beneath the dedicated turn-and-slip dial, but degraded status remains in Diagnostics and on the main horizon. A missing label, healthy sensor status or running notification does not establish attitude accuracy. See held displays and degraded status.

The XCSoar feed sends bank and pitch only. Diagnostics and recordings report actual sensor faults immediately, and the transmitter never uses the display cache as fresh attitude. Only fresh usable calibrated attitude is sent, but degraded estimates may still be transmitted. A SENDING status is not confirmation of reception. XCSoar may retain old values when packets stop; do not assume an automatic fallback.

Another app on the same phone shares its display, power, hardware and operating system. It is not independent hardware redundancy. See connection setup and checks.

Phone and network limitations

Sensor availability, mounting vibration, interrupted background processing and battery restrictions can affect operation. Allowing unrestricted background operation can increase battery consumption and does not guarantee continuity.

Use another-device UDP transmission only on a trusted local network. LAN UDP is not encrypted or authenticated. Bluetooth uses paired devices with Bluetooth Classic SPP, not BLE.

For a problem report, follow the support guidance. These notes describe the experimental application documented on 4 November 2026; consult the instructions supplied with your installed build.
