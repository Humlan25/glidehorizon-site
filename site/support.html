Support & donations

GlideHorizon is developed by Ulf Backudd. Start with the setup guide, and share clear, privacy-conscious problem reports.

Help with an installed build

Check troubleshooting and the operational limitations first. Menu names and features can differ by build.

Use the support channel published with your authorized release. This website does not currently provide an app-support inbox or upload form, and no public app download is offered here.

What to include in a problem report

  • App version and build label, phone model and Android version.
  • Steps to reproduce the problem, expected behavior and what actually happened.
  • The exact visible status or error, including whether readings are held, stale or unavailable.
  • Mounting, calibration and relevant display settings.
  • For connection problems: XCSoar version, connection mode and the receiving device's status.

Use controlled ground tests for reproduction. Remove personal notifications and unrelated private information from screenshots. Review recordings and mounting metadata before sharing; do not post private captures publicly.

Website feedback

The website source is available in the GlideHorizon website repository. Its public availability does not make the Android application's original source open source. Any feedback posted publicly should exclude personal data and private recordings.

Support voluntarily

Contributions are optional and handled by Ko-fi, not by an in-app payment processor. A donation is not required to read this website, does not purchase access to an app build, and does not change the app's measurement capabilities or safety limitations.
